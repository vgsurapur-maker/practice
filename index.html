Method & limitations

Baseline: the average of your previous days for each 2-hour block. With no history, a typical household load shape scaled to your usual daily total is used instead. Anomaly rule: a block is flagged only if it is more than 20% and more than 0.15 kWh above expected. Confidence: 50% for 1 day, 60% for 2, 75% for 3 to 6, 90% for 7 or more. Savings: estimates from fixed formulas, not measured results. AI: it explains only the numbers computed above and cannot confirm which appliance caused a peak.

How it answers

The advisor sees your current baseline, anomalies and action list, explains the evidence, shows uncertainty, and never claims appliance certainty from aggregate data. If the AI service is unavailable, a built-in rule engine answers instead.
