How it works

An intelligent agent: it perceives appliance data, reasons with rules, then acts by advising.

InputAppliance, watts, hours per day, days
AnalysiskWh per appliance and share of total
Prediction30-day projection and bill
Rule engineFirst matching IF–THEN rule fires
OutputRanking, tips, savings

Formulas

kWh = watts × hours × days ÷ 1000share % = appliance kWh ÷ total × 10030-day kWh = watts × hours × 30 ÷ 1000bill = kWh × tariff

Rule base

R1Incandescent bulb → 9 W LED

R2Geyser → use a timer, cut 15 min a day

R3Share ≥ 30% and ≥ 4 h a day → cut 1 hour a day

R4Always on (≥ 20 h) → check settings, about 5%

R5Share ≥ 25% → reduce use by 10%

Educational prototype. Power ratings are treated as constant draw and the tariff is a flat rate, so real bills will differ.
